Where the data come from

X-ray
Real data. Raw EIGER 16M diffraction frames from the dataset deposited with PDB 9QBT (KEAP1), collected at SLS X06SA (PXI); raw data doi:10.18430/M39QBT (proteindiffraction.org). Geometry and spot predictions from the deposited XDS files. The beamline model and Ewald-sphere view are simulated.
Cryo-EM
Simulated images. Atlas, squares, holes and micrographs (CTF, dose, noise) are computed in your browser. Particles are built from PDB 5A1A, E. coli β-galactosidase (UniProt P00722; Bartesaghi et al., Science 2015).
NMR
Simulated spectra computed from real assigned chemical shifts: BMRB 6457 (Cornilescu et al., JACS 1998), human ubiquitin (UniProt P0CG48); structure from PDB 1UBQ.
